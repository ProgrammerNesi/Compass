# NovaTech Products

RoboSense is NovaTech's predictive-maintenance platform. It analyzes vibration and temperature data from industrial machinery to identify potential failures.

FactoryPilot is an autonomous factory-management platform. It coordinates robotic equipment and production scheduling.

RoboSense 2.0 was released in Q3 2024 and introduced anomaly detection using historical equipment patterns.

FactoryPilot 2.0 was released in Q1 2025 and introduced autonomous production scheduling.
